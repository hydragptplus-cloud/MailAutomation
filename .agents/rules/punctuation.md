# Punctuation & Typography Rules

- **Do NOT use em dashes (`—`) or en dashes (`–`) anywhere in code, comments, UI text, labels, titles, or documentation.**
- Use standard punctuation instead:
  - Hyphen (`-`) for separators, fallbacks, or ranges (e.g. `0-100%`, `-`).
  - Colon (`:`) for titles and prefixes (e.g. `Reset password: John`).
  - Parentheses `()` for supplementary info (e.g. `Active (8 backup codes remaining)`).
  - Standard ASCII arrows (`->` or `&rarr;`) for step progressions.
